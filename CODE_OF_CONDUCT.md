# Code of Conduct

This is a technical project. Keep discussion focused on the code and the work.

## Expectations

- Be direct and civil. Critique code, not people.
- Stay on topic. Issues and pull requests are for bugs, features, and review.
- No harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- No spam, off-topic promotion, or publishing others' private information.
- Respect maintainer decisions and the project's design constraints
  (no LLM calls, no MCP, OKF v0.2 conformance).

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or delete content and may block accounts that break
these rules. Report problems to [@TheForgivenOne](https://github.com/TheForgivenOne);
reports are handled privately.
